Architectural Thesis

"We reject the static monument. Buildings must breathe, articulate, and flex against gravity."

Formulated as an architectural laboratory, Structa Studies investigates the threshold where high-tensile engineering meets undulating cast surfaces. Each structure is conceived not as an immovable shelter, but as a calibrated instrument responsive to sunlight, thermal shift, and pedestrian velocity.
